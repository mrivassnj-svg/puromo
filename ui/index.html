⚠ Always verify before powering anything.

          
• Use only on gear you own, on isolated lab networks, or with written authorization.

          
• All logic is 3.3 V — never feed 5 V into GPIO pins.

          
• Never connect a LiPo directly to a Pi's 5 V pin; use a boost/charge board.

          
• Insulate the battery. Add a 1–2 A fuse between battery and boost board.

          
• Check radio-transmission regulations before enabling any transmit-capable module.

          
• Keep a written scope/authorization for any test on systems you don't own.
